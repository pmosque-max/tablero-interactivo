Comparative & Superlative Adjectives

In this lesson, we will describe animals using comparisons:

Key Rule: Short adjectives add -er than (tall -> taller than). Long adjectives use more ... than.

1. The lion is faster than the elephant.

2. The giraffe is the tallest animal in the savanna.
